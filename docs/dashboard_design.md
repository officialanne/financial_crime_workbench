# Financial Crime Executive & Operational Dashboard

## Objective
Provide an operational overview of the institution's financial activity, volume trends, geographical concentrations, and risk exposure to allow analysts to triage threats within 30 seconds.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1. **Operational Metrics:** Total transaction count, cumulative volume (£), average transaction value, maximum transfer.
2. **Risk Metrics:** High, Medium, and Low risk distribution, total alerts raised, open investigations.
3. **Customer Metrics:** Total active customers, high-risk customer count.

## Visualisations
- **Volume & Velocity Trends:** Line chart of monthly transaction volume and transaction count.
- **Risk Distribution:** Bar/Donut chart illustrating Low / Medium / High exposure.
- **Geographic Exposure:** Horizontal bar chart of top originating jurisdictions.
- **Amount Histogram:** Distribution of transaction amounts to spot structuring spikes (e.g., clustered just below £10k).
- **High-Risk Queue Table:** Actionable list of critical transactions requiring immediate investigation.
